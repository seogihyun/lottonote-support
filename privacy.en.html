LottoNote Privacy Policy

Local records

Entered draw records, saved combinations, labels, timestamps and analysis snapshots stay in the app’s local storage. The current app has no operator server, account or cloud synchronization. Its storage directory is configured to be excluded from automatic device backup.

Sharing and files

When you choose to share, only the selected combination and label are supplied to the iOS share sheet. You select the destination and recipient. Copying happens only when you tap the copy button. The app checks only the KakaoTalk URL scheme for availability and does not read contacts. Imports use the system document picker; exported backups include personal records. Files or messages sent to another provider are governed by that provider’s policy.

Retention and deletion

Delete individual combinations or use the app’s delete-all control to remove local records and the internal recovery copy. Exported files and messages in other apps are not removed automatically.

Free draws and rewarded ads

Starting with build 9, two free number generations are available each day, resetting at midnight in Asia/Seoul without rollover. After they are used, completing one opted-in rewarded video grants one additional generation, with no lifetime cap on repeats. An unused earned generation remains until used. The reward is an in-app generation right, not money, a lottery ticket or a prize. Analysis, saving and sharing do not require watching ads.

Advertising and privacy

The advertising feature uses Google AdMob and the UMP consent SDK. Consent status checks connect to Google; ads are requested after the user chooses to watch. Google SDKs may process IP addresses (including approximate location), device or app-scoped identifiers, advertising interactions, crash logs and performance information for advertising, fraud prevention and measurement. Non-personalized ads do not mean no personal data is processed. See Google advertising privacy information.

The app does not attach your entered draws, numbers, labels or saved records to ad requests. It does not request ATT permission to access IDFA. Publisher first-party ID is disabled and non-personalized ads are requested. Firebase, a separate analytics SDK, operator accounts, friend networking and CloudKit synchronization are not used. Required consent forms are shown according to applicable region, with an advertising privacy settings entry when required. Apple diagnostics follow Apple policies.

Usage ledger

Daily usage, unused earned credit and reward deduplication values stay in a separate on-device ledger excluded from exported backups. Deleting number records or restoring their backup does not reset the ledger; uninstalling removes this local storage. Skipped or failed ads grant no credit and consume no existing generation. Development builds use Google test ads; live monetization remains subject to account and release readiness.

Contact

Contact: seogihyun@gmail.com. If you choose to email support, your sender address and message are delivered to this support mailbox. The app does not send support emails automatically. Do not send passwords, authentication codes or a complete private record history. Email handling by the email provider follows that provider’s policy.

Website hosting

This privacy and support site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. The operator has not added analytics scripts, ads or input forms to this site. See GitHub Pages documentation for details.
