LottoNote Support

Generate and save

Open 번호 생성 and select 번호 뽑기. Watch the animation or skip to the result, then name and save your combination.

Analyze records

Enter ten consecutive records in 분석. Each requires six distinct values from 1 through 45. Current winning results are not downloaded automatically. The sample file contains synthetic data, not real winning results.

Sharing

Select an installed app through the iOS share sheet or copy the numbers. For KakaoTalk, installation and sign-in may be needed. Choose the recipient yourself.

Backups

Use settings to export and restore backups. Keep exported files private. Deleting app records does not remove files or messages in other providers.

Contact

Prepare the device model, iOS version, app version and reproduction steps. Do not send passwords, authentication codes or a complete private record history. Email support at seogihyun@gmail.com. Sending an email remains your choice.
